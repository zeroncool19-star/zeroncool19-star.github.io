Brick Builder World

An open island to explore and build on. Snap together bricks, plates, slopes, arches, windows, doors and more. Build anything you like.

📱 Touch controls are on. Turn your phone sideways for the best view.

Controls

🕹 Left joystick – walk (push far to run)
👆 Drag the screen – look around
👆 Tap – place a brick where you tap
✖ Remove mode – then tap bricks to remove them
✋ Press and hold a brick – copy it
⬆ Jump / fly up · ⬇ fly down · 🕊 fly on/off
↻ Rotate · ↶ Undo · 🧱 All bricks & colours
Tap the hotbar at the top to switch bricks
